# Problem

A long recording can exhaust Nemotron's eight local channels even when fewer than eight people speak at once. A later voice can then inherit an earlier channel's person assignment. Channel identity and person identity have different lifetimes. The live adapter also discarded old activity and retained only the latest channel embedding, while People kept the first three playable examples. That evidence cannot support complete after-recording regrouping.

The accepted scope assumes capacity-related confusion begins after eight established channels. Continuous identity re-verification below that limit is outside this change. The work targets future recordings; it does not modify existing private meetings during development.

# Design

## Evidence and identity

Preserve each successfully generated voice embedding as meeting evidence, with an immutable sample identifier, exact embedding compatibility identity, audio source, local speaker identity, and absolute start/end times. Preserve compact speaker-activity intervals for the full recording. Recording remains responsible for bounded audio buffering and inference scheduling. Embedding evidence does not contain PCM; its time range points to the retained recording.

Local identities are scoped to a source and Nemotron generation. Clusters are meeting-wide acoustic groups. People are reviewed named identities and may own multiple clusters, including different microphones or call conditions. No matching of channel numbers across generations implies a shared person. All evidence remains private and is excluded from text exports.

An append-only meeting evidence journal separates durable observations from the replaceable transcript tail. Finish drains accepted embedding work and the evidence writer before consolidation can start. Invalid records and incomplete final writes must have explicit recovery behavior; a damaged completed record must not silently become an empty successful result.

## Live capacity rollover

Establishment uses sustained channel activity rather than the eight identities allocated at startup, and is sticky within one window. On reaching capacity, request a nearby handoff, with a bounded wait during continuous speech. Reuse the loaded model and reset only its per-source streaming state. Bootstrap from bounded recent PCM without resetting the ongoing recording, transcript service, or continuous resampling timeline.

One timestamp T owns the handoff: old-generation output ends at T and new-generation publication begins at T. Bootstrap speech before T supplies context and cannot republish old activity. New local identities use fresh embedding evidence. Gap resets remain distinct from capacity rollover. A saturated bootstrap cannot repeatedly reset without a rearming condition that can create headroom.

## Consolidation and reconstruction

After recording, associate trusted local channels across windows rather than clustering each short embedding independently. The accepted assumption is that Nemotron's channel identity remains usable below capacity. The experiment rejected sample-level grouping because it split reliable local identities and increased errors. The final engine therefore keeps each trusted source/local channel as one indivisible unit.

Trust requires explicit capture provenance: source, generation, local IDs, publication start, observed end, first capacity timestamp, and the rollover policy revision. A window is trusted from publication start up to the earlier of observed end and first capacity. The interval between capacity and rollover is retained but unresolved. A bootstrap already at capacity supplies no trusted continuation after handoff. Missing, unknown-policy, or contradictory provenance cannot grant trust. Old journals remain readable, but source-file completion alone cannot qualify them for this method.

Only corrected `community1SpeechSpan` v2 samples fully inside the trusted range contribute to a channel unit. Normalize the mean of its valid embeddings, then perform deterministic complete-link agglomerative clustering of units with an initial cosine threshold of 0.72. Every cross-unit pair in a proposed merge must pass the threshold. Different channels with overlapping activity on the same source cannot merge. A unit's sample-to-mean dispersion is recorded for audit; it is not a calibrated confidence score. The threshold is an evaluated engineering choice, not a probability of shared identity.

The complete retained activity timeline supplies speech boundaries. A sampled trusted unit receives its group's identity across that trusted activity, including speech between samples. No fixed 15-second propagation horizon is used. Units without compatible samples, intervals outside trusted windows, and ambiguous provenance remain unresolved. Record directly sampled activity, activity inferred from trusted channel continuity, and unresolved activity separately. This reconstruction preserves the model's speech detection errors; clustering cannot create missed speech or remove false alarms.

Transcript publication preserves words, timing, source, and explicit corrections. It requires at least 60% row coverage and a runner-up below 25% of the winner's support. Any overlap with an unresolved interval preserves the whole row, preventing a mostly trusted sentence from carrying a new identity across the capacity boundary. This is conservative because rows have no word-level timing. An unchanged one-to-one group preserves its original local UUID, label, and name only when all of that local identity’s activity is resolved. Changed membership creates a stable membership-derived ID without inheriting names by channel number.

Publication uses the existing canonical queue and rollback journal. Review examples, result artifacts, prior transcript history, meeting labels, and task completion receipt commit together. Validate the original input receipt and exact prior history bytes before writing, then validate inputs again before commit. Apply current review decisions inside the queue and reserve voice writes even for unchanged retries. Cancellation before canonical admission keeps the existing result; after admission the atomic transaction finishes, and a committed receipt takes precedence over a late cancellation request.

Retained evidence avoids a mandatory second full inference pass. **Analyze Recording** remains the complete saved-audio pipeline for missing or insufficient evidence. Automatic targeted re-analysis is not implemented. The integrated trusted-window engine and publication policy require their own replay and application checks; earlier channel-unit candidate results alone do not validate the stricter policy.

## Person profiles and review examples

Meeting evidence is not capped at three examples. A person's routine matching profile uses a bounded representative selection per compatible model, initially at most twelve vectors. Selection favors usable speech and acoustic diversity across reviewed groups rather than recency or arrival order. Multiple acoustic groups may belong to one person; no single global centroid should erase recording-condition differences. Retain the reviewed group decisions and underlying evidence independently of this matching budget.

Choose a few representative playable examples per consolidated cluster for People review. An automatic suggestion does not enroll a sample. Reassignment and explicit removal must remove the previous contribution. Selection budgets and clustering thresholds are engineering parameters to evaluate, not calibrated identity probabilities.

# Implemented solution

- `LocalLiveDiarization` and `LiveSpeakerCapacity` retain per-source replay audio and establish capacity from three continuous seconds of filtered activity. Rollover waits for 300 ms of silence or at most five seconds, replays at most 45 seconds, and clips publication at the received-audio handoff. New generations use new local identities. Saturated bootstrap rearming requires an earlier recent channel to leave the replay horizon.
- `SpeakerEvidenceStore` journals all accepted activity and successfully returned timed embeddings independently of the People example limit. Records are versioned, bounded to 4 MiB, and saved with private permissions. A torn final record can be inspected but does not qualify as complete; committed corruption is an error. Stop drains producers and writes a terminal marker. `SpeakerEvidenceInputReceipt` binds completion to finalized audio and the journal using the app's size-and-modification-time revision convention.
- `SpeakerConsolidation` implements the final channel-unit method described above, with explicit trusted cutoffs and separate direct, inferred, and unresolved activity counts. Its matrix scales with channel units rather than all samples; storage is quadratic in units, and the straightforward agglomerative search can be expensive for very many generations. Cancellation is checked during the work. The rejected sample-level engine is isolated under the experiment for reproducible comparisons; it is not a parallel production path.
- `MeetingSpeakerConsolidation` retains stable group identities, allocates distinct labels for changed groups, unions overlapping activity before voting, and preserves rows touching unresolved intervals or manual decisions. Version 2 result receipts record the exact input receipt, grouping configuration, and audit information rather than presenting model scores as certainty.
- Publication extends the existing canonical library transaction: staged unreviewed voice examples, meeting labels, completion receipt, prior transcript history, and result artifacts commit together. Save failure restores both files and in-memory state; crash recovery uses the existing rollback journal and preserves private file permissions. Supplemental artifacts reject unsafe paths and symlinks. Analysis binds to the original finalized source receipt and revalidates before writing and committing, so resealing replacement inputs cannot authorize stale results. History is prepared from the same prior bytes that the transaction validates. Unchanged retries still reserve voice writes until publication finishes, and the canonical queue reapplies current review decisions before staging. Retired live callbacks recheck their generation after journal I/O so they cannot publish into a subsequent recording. No parallel transaction framework or automatic sample enrollment was added.
- The durable task reuses the Speaker Labeling queue, mutual exclusion, retry, cancellation, result binding, and labeling history. Automatic consolidation honors **Automatically Label Speakers**. Queued tasks survive recording deferral; disabled automatic settings and explicit cancellation remain authoritative. Missing or stale retained evidence uses the existing saved-audio path rather than silently accepting changed inputs.
- `VoiceProfileSelection` selects a central example followed by acoustically diverse examples, with a small duration/quality preference. Routine matching uses at most twelve confirmed representatives per compatible model and person; scores use the best matching mode and a margin between people. Several groups may belong to one person. Fresh matches become review suggestions, not automatically enrolled samples. Existing exact-audio review decisions are applied before publishing new labels.
- The Speakers panel uses `canReviewVoice` and is absent when no label has a valid embedding. **Label Speakers** exposes **Consolidate Speakers** and **Analyze Recording** when completed evidence is available. Both adopt a recovered transcript before processing it. The synthetic Preview fixture exercises merging, splitting, missing evidence, task publication, and history.
- Portable exports and website archives omit fingerprints, review references, and evidence sidecars. Existing provider/model and search changes from the other sessions remain in the integrated checkout; their completion and final validation are checked before commit.

# Reasoning

The user prefers the labeling model's local continuity while capacity remains available. Channel-unit consolidation respects that boundary and uses fingerprints to connect local identities across generations and sources. Retaining all successful observations allows representative selection and later evaluation without repeatedly analyzing the recording. Keeping this evidence separate from bounded review examples and person profiles controls routine matching cost without discarding the acoustic record.

The earlier independent-sample method failed evaluation and is not the final publication design. Reusing task, history, review, and canonical persistence infrastructure avoids a second transaction or assignment system. The corrected short-span WeSpeaker extractor has its own compatibility identity; older embeddings remain readable but cannot silently mix with corrected ones.

# Risks and technical debt

No temporary publication transaction, automatic enrollment shortcut, or inferred channel-number provenance is introduced. The following limitations are explicit, with their consequences and follow-up work:

- **Existing live evidence coverage:** short or overlapping speech, busy extraction, bootstrap, and disabled association can leave a channel without usable samples. Retain all successful observations, report unresolved activity, and preserve affected rows. Full saved-audio analysis is available. Measure real concurrent recording coverage before designing targeted re-analysis; accelerated replay does not cover busy skips.
- **Identity assumption:** below-capacity trust is the accepted scope, not proof that Nemotron never confuses voices. Channel averaging must stop at explicit capacity boundaries. Continued evaluation across languages, microphones, overlap, and longer recordings is needed before stronger reliability claims. Continuous live re-verification remains outside this change.
- **Existing speech detection:** grouping leaves missed speech, false alarms, and activity timing errors unchanged. The observed high false-alarm recording demonstrates this limit. Improving segmentation or running complete saved-audio diarization is separate from identity grouping.
- **Existing correction granularity:** manual assignments apply to a whole local label. Preserve them even if that label later held another voice. A later time-local correction/conflict review should let users repair that case without silently changing explicit decisions.
- **Existing transcript timing:** rows lack word timestamps. Rows crossing unresolved boundaries or conflicting groups stay unchanged. Word alignment and narrower transcript edits are required to improve sentence-internal attribution.
- **Existing storage revision contract:** size and modification time detect ordinary changes, not same-size replacements that preserve timestamps. Inputs are bound to the original receipt throughout the task. A future library-wide content-revision policy is required for cryptographic integrity; this feature does not claim it.
- **Scale:** capture PCM is bounded and journal records are bounded, but retained evidence and after-recording working memory grow with meeting duration. The trusted engine keeps a unit-pair matrix and currently uses a straightforward complete-link search. Profile large multi-hour, multi-source recordings before optimizing that search. Person matching selects at most twelve vectors per compatible model but hydrates reviewed examples first; cache selection by review and representation revision if measured library size makes this costly.
- **Compatibility:** old evidence without trustworthy window metadata cannot be retroactively declared trusted. Keep it readable and offer saved-audio analysis; do not fabricate boundaries. Old and corrected embedding contracts remain separate. Existing provider/model migration details remain in their own worklog.
