## Original problems

1. Recording used about 90–108% CPU. The original profile in `scratchpad/recording-sample.txt` showed the main thread busy in SwiftUI graph updates in 7,662 of 7,672 samples. The target is under 20% CPU.
2. The global `MeetingStore.isBusy` disabled New Recording during transcription. Recording should be disabled only while a recording is starting, running, or saving, or when the library is read-only.

The two fixes share `MeetingStore.swift` and are included in the same validated integration.
